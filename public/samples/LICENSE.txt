BeatBlock procedural drum samples

The WAV files in this directory are original PCM clips generated by the project's
scripts/generate_original_samples.py from deterministic mathematical waveforms.
They contain no third-party sample-pack material or external recordings.

copy, modify, distribute, and use them, including commercially, without asking
permission or providing attribution. Full legal code:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

The generator script is part of the BeatBlock source tree and is not itself
covered by this audio-asset dedication.
